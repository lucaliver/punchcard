# Punchcard — mechanics reference

Read the section of the mechanic you are about to touch; [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) has the rules for every change.
Field-by-field details live in the doc comments of `src/game/types.ts` and of the modules named here: this file only
tells how the pieces of a mechanic fit together across files. Keep it true: a change that makes a line stale fixes it in the same commit.

## Run and rewards

- **A run is a graph drawn as an office floor plan.** `RunNode.next[]` + `lane`; per act (`ACT_DEFS`): a shared opening (act 1: two fights, then always a special room), two
  lanes linked a couple of times and now and then with one road cut (`LANES`, `LINKS`, `CONFIG.roadCut` in `run.ts`), then the boss, which leads to the next act. Only the rooms reachable next are in sight (`VISION`, `journey.ts`, plus the Floor Plan's `mods.vision`; the boss always is), the rest is fogged.
- **Scripted acts.** The first time a player meets an act its rooms and enemies are fixed (`ACT_SCRIPTS`, `SCRIPTED_ACTS` in `run.ts`: a hand-drawn graph of rooms, enemies and one-way roads per act, built by `addScripted`; act 1 on the very first run, acts 2 and 3 the first time each is reached; `meta.actsReached`, `newRun(…, scripted)` takes the acts to script). Rewards are never scripted, and the other acts are dealt like any run's. `dev/map-editor.html` (dev server) edits these scripts: it reads `ACT_SCRIPTS`, the enemies and the sprites from the game's own modules, so it is always in step, and Esporta prints the new `ACT_SCRIPTS` entries to paste into `run.ts` (the owner sends them to Claude).
- **The weekend (the bonus act).** `MAIN_ACTS` (`data/acts.ts`, 3) is a full workday: `fullDay`, `weekendOpen` and the `allStamped` hero unlocks count it, never `ACT_DEFS.length`. The acts after it are a bonus a run only has when its hero has won a full day (`weekendOpen`) (`newRun(…, acts)`: `main.ts` passes `ACT_DEFS.length` then; `parseRun` accepts either count). At the foot of the last main boss (`atWeekendFork`, `goJourney` in `main.ts`) a window offers the weekend or *Clock out for good* (`endRun` as a win: `fullDay`); the last boss of the map, the one with nothing after it, is the *Final Boss* (`nodeKind`, `journey.ts`). Beating a bonus act's boss stamps it (`hero:4`) and counts a `weekends` record. An act without elites or boss of its own deals the previous act's (`enemiesFor`), so the weekend can grow one enemy at a time; its reward, upgrade and stationery tables have their own column (`config.ts`).
- **Rooms.** `NodeType`: fight, elite, boss, rest, promotion, copy, … each a screen in `ROOMS` (`main.ts`). The Tailor and Restructuring are dealt once per run (`ONCE_PER_RUN`, `run.ts`). A new room = `NodeType`, `LANES` entry, `ROOMS` screen, `NODE_ICON`, `journey.node.*`/`journey.info.*` strings, and a picture: a sprite `room.<type>` in `art/rooms.ts` plus a `ROOM_SCENE` entry (its motion is a class in `rooms.css`) that the screen shows with `roomScene(type)` (the Raffle's picture is its own wheel, built by the screen).
- **Restructuring** (`restructuring` room, `ui/screens/restructuring.ts`; `canRestructure`, `restructure`, `giftRelic` in `run.ts`; `CONFIG.restructureCards`). Two ways: *Lateral Move* has the player pick `restructureCards` deck cards (`openDeck` with `count`/`onPickMany`: every copy is its own card) that each turn into a random other card, its rarity dealt from the elite reward odds of the act (`rewardOdds('elite', act)`; `rewardPool` of the hero's class and neutral, so no basic card; never the same card), always from scratch: upgrade and perks are lost; *Exit Package* gives a random stationery (`rollRelics(run, 1)`, one the run doesn't hold) and leaves the deck alone. Curses and special cards can't be moved. Nothing is saved until the room ends, like the other rooms.
- **Raffle** (`wheel` room, `ui/screens/wheel.ts`; `WHEEL` in `data/wheel.ts`; `spinWheel`, `applyWheel`, `canWheel`, `wheelTargets` in `run.ts`; `CONFIG.wheelCards`, `wheelSpinTime`, `wheelTurns`). A wheel of `WheelSlot`s, each one effect on a deck card: an upgrade or a perk (`effect.perk`, named by `perk.<id>`). The perks are `PERKS` ones, and a `bad` perk (Overtime: costs more; Red Tape: `pending`; Temp Contract: `fleeting`) is a drawback that the Promotion never offers: only the wheel hands it out. The result comes from `spinWheel` (dealt from a fresh `Rng` at every spin, so the same run state lands on a different slice each time; a slice no deck card could take is never dealt; if none can, the button just ends the room) and the picture only follows it: the disc is a bitmap painted pixel by pixel (`paintDisc`) that the screen's `frame` turns in `TURN_STEP` degrees, a tick and a flick of the pointer at each seam. Then the button (*Choose 3 cards*) opens the deck (`openDeck` with `count` and `previewSelected`; it never opens by itself): the player picks `wheelCards` cards (or as many as fit) and `applyWheel` puts the slice on them, each shown with `playCardChange`. The wheel is the room's picture, so the room has no sprite and no `ROOM_SCENE` entry. A perk's cost mark is yellow when it lowers the cost and red (`.dear`) when it raises it.
- **Map layouts** (`dealLayout`, `run.ts`) are built clean: a link or a cut that would give a room two choices of the same kind is not a candidate, so nothing is dealt again.
- **Enemies avoid the last run's** (`meta.lastEnemies`, set by `startRun`, passed to `newRun(…, avoid)`): normal fights are drawn from the bag with those at the back, elites and bosses pick among the others while any is left.
- **Rewards**: a normal fight offers `REWARD_CHOICES.fight` cards (three), elites and bosses one more (`rewardChoices(run, kind)`, plus the rank's perks and stationery); `rollRewards` deals each card from `rewardOdds`, but the first `rewardGuarantee(kind, act)` cards come from that rarity or above; the offer is sorted by rarity, then cost. Each card comes already upgraded with the chance `rewardUpgradeChance(rarity, act)` (the rarer, the less often; more every act). The Vending Machine's first option drops `CONFIG.vendingCards.rare` different rare cards at once. Within a rarity the hero's own class weighs `CONFIG.classCardWeight` against neutral cards (`pickReward`, also used by the Vending Machine). The Rolodex (`RelicDef.openPool`) opens every class: `rewardPool(…, anyClass)` returns all cards but curses, and `pickReward` weighs each card by 1 / the cards its class has in the pool, so each class and the neutral cards are equally likely; the Vending Machine, the skip bonus and Restructuring use the same pool. Skipping a reward pays max HP (`skipPay`): `CONFIG.skipMaxHp` the first time, one less for each skip after it (`RunState.skips`), never under 1.
- **The reward on offer is saved** (`RunState.reward`, set by `offerReward` after a fight, dropped by `nextNode`): Continue reopens the reward screen with the same cards instead of the map.
- **Run history**: `meta.history` (`RunLog`, newest `CONFIG.historyMax`), written by `finishRun`/`abandonRun` with the final deck and stationery; the handbook's History page lists it and a tap opens the run's payslip, stationery and deck (`runDetail.ts`). The handbook turns pages with a sideways swipe (`PAGES` in `compendium.ts`). The end screen's three bars (cards, enemies, stationery) fill from zero to `collection().n` of `total`, ticking (`tally`) at each step, with a `+k` stamp for what the run added (`n - from`): `meta.base` is the handbook's tally taken when the run starts (`startingFirstRun`), the handbook's own counters read the same `collection()`, and tapping a bar opens the handbook on its section (`compendiumScreen(onBack, open)`).
- **Beg to stay**: the first time a run's hero would lose a fight (`CombatSetup.canBeg`, once per run through the saved `relicFlags[BEG_FLAG]`) the engine freezes (`Combat.begging`, event `beg`) and the UI asks; `Combat.beg(accept)` answers (`CONFIG.beg`). The balance bot never begs.
- **Life Insurance** (the Necromancer's power): a hero status with `StatusDef.onDeath` is asked first when the hero would fall (`Combat.insured`, before relics and the beg): the status heals `v` HP, `Combat.consumed` takes the policy's deck card out of the run (`Combat.mem.policyUid`, not for a stolen temp copy) and `Combat.loseSleeveSlots` adds to `run.sleeveLost` (saved; `CombatSetup.sleeveLost` takes it off the sleeve from the next fight, never the last slot).

## Cards

- **Falling cards**: a belt card past the end gets `BeltCard.falling` (`CONFIG.fallGrace` s, also the CSS `--fall-grace`): it tips over (`.falling`) but can still be grabbed and stashed; only then is it lost (`expire`). A hero with `HeroDef.fallDiscount` (the Rogue's Sticky Fingers) makes every falling card take that much off the cost of one random card in the sleeve (`Combat.cheapenRandom`; `CombatCard.disc`: mana off its cost until it is played; `cardCostOf` includes it, `fullCostOf` doesn't: what Fire Sale and Expense It read). `Combat.dropBelt()` makes the whole belt fall; Stocktake (the Rogue's ability) drops it with `dropFront` and gains mana equal to the original costs it returns. `HeroDef.manaOverflow` (Sticky Fingers' second part) lets `Combat.gainMana` go above max mana, up to the mana cap (the regen still stops at the max; the bar grows extra pips). Clean Getaway counts falls in its status's `e` and gives Block every `CLEAN_GETAWAY_EVERY`th. `Combat.dropFront(n)` (Cut the Wire) makes the `n` oldest fall and returns what they were worth (`frontWorth`); Written Off reads `stats.cardsLost`. A Fleeting card that falls is used up through `exhaustCard`, so the `onExhaust` statuses (Shredder, Grief Counseling) see it: an Unplayable + Fleeting card with an `onExpire` (the Necromancer's way to pile up the exhaust pile) pays off when it rides off the belt. **Steal warning**: `Combat.stealTarget()` marks the card an enemy's steal move is about to take for the last `CONFIG.stealWarn` s (a yellow hand over it).
- **Last stretch of the belt**: past `0.86` of its run a card blinks (`.leaving`) when leaving does something (an `onExpire`) or when it is Fleeting and can still be played; the Fleeting one also trembles (`.last-chance`): its last chance. Pinned cards and unplayable Fleeting ones (Intern, Quick Sync) don't.
- **The exhaust pile** (`Combat.exhaust`, what Layoffs counts): cards played with Exhaust or Consume, Fleeting ones that fell, sleeve cards scrapped, and played powers (those last go in without `onExhaust`). `Combat.exhume()` (Reopen Closed Tab) plays a random one for free and uses it up again: never a power, a consumed card or one a rule bars. `StatusDef.bonusDamage` adds damage to the hero's cards from a status (Offshore Account: mana above the max).
- Cost, keywords and values of a copy always come from `cardCostOf`/`cardKeywordsOf`/`cardValsOf` (upgrades and **perks** included); in a fight the cost is `Combat.costOf`/`cardCost`, which a `flatCost` status can override.
- A card that branches on the fight's state (`{?poison}`, `{?block}`…) gives `CardDef.when` from `WHEN` (`data/cards/conditions.ts`) and its `play` reads the same function: the belt and sleeve faces light the condition up (`.cond.met`) while it holds.
- A card whose numbers move with the fight (Dress Code's Block, Raise Denied's damage) gives `CardDef.shown` (the same maths its `play` uses): the belt and sleeve faces read `Combat.shownVals`, so they show what the card would do right now.
- A card's `desc` names another card with `[@id]` (rendered as its name): in the detail view it is bold, coloured and tappable (opens that card; a test checks the ids exist).
- Curse *cards* live in `neutral.ts` (their rarity is a power level: common = a nuisance, rare = hurts or clogs, epic = shuts down belt space or can't be cleared; they never drop as rewards and can't be upgraded). A curse's `onExpire` can reach past the hero's HP: Pink Slip takes the sleeve's last slot for the fight (`Combat.dropSleeveSlot`: its card moves to a free slot, or to the discard pile; one slot always stays), Hot Mic strengthens the enemy, Out of Coffee chills the hero (Chill also slows the hero's mana), and an `unplayable` curse with `bonusIdx` (Death March) can only be stashed in the sleeve. **Hexes** (`hexes.ts`) are a different thing: a curse on one belt card, chipped away by taps, on the belt or in the sleeve; a hex with `stages`, like Crumple, shows the card as a picture instead of the stone (`.card.pictured`): one icon per tap left.
- Keywords `echo` (a played card stays where it is: `cardEchoed`) and `anchor` (it stops pinned at `ANCHOR_POS` instead of falling) are engine ones; the handbook's Keywords page lists `KEYWORD_LIST` (a test keeps it equal to the `kw.*` strings).
- **Sushi order**: the Sushi Chef's Omakase is a chore (below) that works on the belt itself. Its plates are temp cards of `CardDef.sushi` (`'piece'`: can be in the order; `'trap'`: the fugu, never in it) that `Combat.spawnCard` deals instead of the deck while `Chore.serve` answers (`SushiOrder.serve`), and a tap on one is `Combat.eat`; they can't be stashed or played, and one that rides off the belt is just gone (the order deals it again).
- **Statuses that bend another rule**: `StatusDef.keeps` stops another status running out (a timed one's clock, a `dot`'s stacks: Red Tape holds the enemy's Poison), `barsSleeve` puts the sleeve out of reach like a sunk one (`Combat.lowerBarred`: Batch Processing), `onStruck` fires on every hit that lands, Block or not, never for damage over time (Good Vibes Only, Grudge Ledger; `onHurt` is only for lost HP). Performance Plan's status stretches the enemy's Vulnerable in place (`PLAN_CAP`). `firstPlayCut` takes mana off a card the first time its id is played in a fight (`Combat.costOf`, `fightPlayed`: Introductory Offer); `hireCut` marks every card added during the fight (`addTempCard`, never a curse) with `CombatCard.welcome`, mana off until it is played (`cardCostOf`: Signing Bonus); `dotFloor` keeps the enemy's damage-over-time status from losing a stack under the status's amount (Pre-Existing Condition). `crit` is a stack count, one per attack card played (Zero-Day Exploit gives several). Pile cards: `Combat.playCheapest` (Fuck You Money: plays and exhausts the cheapest cards of the draw and discard piles), `upgradeRandom` (Scope Creep: an upgrade of the draw and discard piles for the fight; with `forGood`, Mentorship Program: any deck card, kept in `Combat.upgraded` and written to the run by `applyCombat`), `cleanse` (Clean Slate, Beg to stay), `loseCrystals` (Voluntary Redundancy).
- **The office batch's piles and clocks**: `Combat.playRandom('draw' | 'discard', n)` plays random cards of a pile for free (Vibe Coding, Cron Job; they land in the discard pile), `swapSleeve` turns every sleeve card into a made-up one of the same rarity from any class (White Elephant), `pinOldest` tapes the belt card nearest the exit (Duct Tape, `BeltCard.pinned`), `deckSize` counts the run's cards in the fight (Pitch Deck), `moveSecondsLeft` the whole seconds before the enemy's move lands (Under Pressure) and `printedCost` a card's price without discounts (Skim Off the Top). Days Without an Accident keeps the Strength it gave in `Combat.mem.daysGained` so a hurt can take exactly that back; Four-Day Week hands out a Lost Badge stack every `v` cards; Demo Day is a `flatCost` status of 0 whose `onEnd` stops the belt. Strength is `signed`: Boris and Class Struggle take it below zero (a negative stack weakens attacks and stays until it is exactly 0).
- **Tenure** (`CardDef.tenure`: Seniority, Tenure): every play of a deck copy adds one to `CardInst.tenure` (`Combat.tenured` → `applyCombat` writes it back to the run, saved with the deck), and `cardValsOf` raises `vals[i]` by `vals[by]` per play up to `vals[to]`, so the face, the deck view and the fight all agree. A card made up in a fight (`temp`) earns none.
- **Heist cards** (the Rogue's Lupin, Catch Me If You Can, The Italian Job, Ocean's Eleven) call `Combat.loot(rarity, n, { up, cheaper, hex })`: it lifts cards of that rarity from the other heroes (never the hired hero's, the neutral ones, or a hero still in the works) into the draw pile, crumpled. So no card effect may live in a hero's own hooks: a stolen card has to work for any hero (Plague and Virulent Form are statuses for that reason).
- A fight can give a copy something its deck card doesn't have: `CombatCard.fleeting` (Krusty Krab's copies), read through `cardKeywordsOf`; `addTempCard(…, extra)` carries it.
- `large` cards (carry-on, duty free, snow globe) are 2 wide with a `wide` icon; the belt centres the art over both halves (`.card.joined > .c-art`) and the sleeve shows it in one. A large card's painting (`CARD_ART`, shown instead of the icon with the Card art debug switch) is wide the same way, placed by the same rules (`.cart.wide`).

## Enemies, heroes, statuses

- `EnemyDef` (`enemies.ts`): needs a sprite in `creatures.ts` (an enemy with `onHalf` also needs its angrier `halfArt` sprite, `<id>Angry`: the base drawing plus `rage(…)`; a test checks it), `enemy.<id>.name`, `move.<id>` per move, `enemy.<id>.half` if it has `onHalf`, and a `note` (an icon id, with `enemy.<id>.note`) when nothing else tells before the fight what it does: every enemy has a pre-fight line unless its trait is a surprise (a test checks it). Global difficulty: `CONFIG.enemyHp`/`enemyDmg`; floor scaling `CONFIG.floorHp`/`floorDmg`. The run's adaptive pressure scales every enemy on top (see Adaptive difficulty). `DEBUG_ENEMY` is in `ENEMIES` but in no list: only the debug fight menu offers it. `ruleBreaker` marks enemies that bend belt or play rules: act 2's first fight is always one (`addAct`, `run.ts`).
- **The hero's ability is once per act.** `Combat.abilitySpent` (set by `useAbility`, read by `abilityReady`) is seeded from `RunState.abilityAct === node.act` in `combatSetup`, and `applyCombat` writes the act back when a fight used it: the next act's fights start with it ready again. The belt's button is dimmed (`.spent`) and says so on a tap or a hold; the hero sheet and the hero select say *Once per shift*. The test bot keeps it for elites, bosses and HP under `BOT_ABILITY_HP`. A relic with `RelicDef.abilityEveryFight` (the Gold Watch) has it ready again at every fight: `combatSetup` never seeds it spent (`abilityEveryFight`, `data/relics.ts`), and the texts say *Once per fight* (`Combat.abilityEveryFight`).
- `HeroDef` (`heroes.ts`): needs a card file, a sprite, `hero.<id>.*` strings, `ABILITY_ICON`/`PASSIVE_ICON` entries (`ui/combat/view.ts`). Starter decks have `startUpgraded` copies and the first Coffee has the Innate perk (`STARTER_PERKS`). Optional `unlock` (checked by `progress()` in `meta.ts`): `finishRun`, `reachBoss`, `allStamped` (the Rogue: every other hero has beaten that act's boss; `progress()` checks the stamps on every call). A hero still in the works has `unlock: { debug: true }`: only the debug menu's unlock-all hires them (`progress()` never does), and until then `heroHidden`/`cardHidden` (`meta.ts`) keep them and all their cards out of the hero select, the handbook, cross-training and the debug fight menu. Cross-training (`rollCrossTraining`) offers no card of a hero not yet hired.
- `StatusDef` (`statuses.ts`): `selfName` — the hero's side reads under `status.<id>.self` (Stun on the hero is *Asleep*, keyword `[asleep]`: use `statusName`/`statusDesc`, `cardView.ts`); `progress` + `cue` draw a status with a trigger as a filling bar and play a sound when `Combat.cue` fires; `look` also tints the hero's portrait and, on the hero's side, the belt (Slowdown, Stalled, Hurry and Crunch wear caution tape on it). Strings: `status.<id>` and `status.<id>.d` (`{v}` = amount).
- Its `tone` (required: red force, green healing, toxic yellow-green poison, teal defence, amber speed and time, purple rules and control, blue mana and tech) is its colour everywhere: chip, drain bar, floater, keyword text, move chips. An enemy move takes its own from what it does (`moveTone`, `moveText.ts`: Snark poisons, so it is green). Inks are `--tone-*` in `tokens.css`, read through `[data-tone]` as `var(--tone)`/`var(--tone-hi)`: tag the element, never name a colour per status or intent.
- `passive: true` marks a permanent enemy trait. `hidden: true` keeps a trait a surprise (no chip, no pre-fight line; the handbook still lists it, as it does the `deepBelt`/`beltOff`/`manaTap` and `halfSecret` surprises: `enemyTraits(e, inFight)`).
- A status can put a window over the belt (`StatusDef.popup`, The Nerd's Update Needed): the engine keeps `Combat.popup` and covers every belt card while it is up (`isCovered`), the HUD shows `.update-popup` and its two buttons call `startUpdate`/`postponeUpdate`; the test bot postpones.
- A status with `StatusDef.beltSparks` (Multitasking) flickers two sparks per stack above and below the belt.
- **Act 3 rules (night shift)** are statuses (`statuses.ts`): `microsleep`, `rateLimit` (`capsHits`), `assemblyLine` (`canPlay`), `overtimeCreep`, `lowBattery`, `machineLearning`, `vipTreatment` (`critOnDrag`: the VIP Client turns an attack the hero drags onto the stage critical, a tap doesn't: `Combat.playCard(uid, 'tap' | 'drag' | 'auto')`; `handsTied` on Stun: no play or ability by hand, stashing still works, while a card slipping off still plays itself under Autopilot: the Factory Siren's song puts both on you). `music.ts` has `combat3`/`map3` for the act (act 2's map is the sunny `map2`); its clock runs past midnight (`shift: [22, 30]`).

## Enemy mechanics in a fight

- **Enemy head start**: the enemy's first move starts `CONFIG.enemyDelay` seconds late (`EnemyState.timer` begins below zero, so the threat bar waits and the countdown shows the extra time); belt, mana and the rest run from the first tick.
- **Different cards played** (`Combat.uniquePlayed('fight' | 'run')`, Case File, Rap Sheet): `fightPlayed` and `runPlayed` collect the ids of the cards played (a card counts itself). The run keeps its set in `RunState.playedIds` (`CombatSetup.playedIds` in, `applyCombat` writes it back).
- **Enemy on stage**: a normal enemy walks in the moment the door is behind us (`doorDone` calls `enterScene`, `.stage.entering`: its shadow grows, then it drops in; the first-fight tour and the sleeve hint wait for it to land; in that very first fight Clock in is hidden until the tour's last step). Before Clock in, its passives sit on a slip *above* the button (`.foe-traits`, headed by the enemy's face and *Enemy passive*). The sprite is sized to the room from the ground up to the top bar's text, which it may stand over (`layout`, `combatScreen.ts`). Under the move's bar, a thin bar (`.guard`) shows the hero's Block against the hit being charged: the bar is the whole hit, the teal what Block covers (it keeps its room when no hit is coming). When a boss's belt loses a row the room grows and `layout` measures again: the sprite steps up to the new size (`.grows`). The hero's HP bar is green, the enemy's pink; a boss's bar has a tick where each phase starts (`.phase-tick`, dimmed once passed). Until Clock in the belt and its cards are dark (`.combat.waiting`). a boss walks in with the office lights out instead (the stage is `.unseen` while the door opens, so it is not on show until the blackout): `.boss-intro` is a black curtain whose tubes flicker (`bi-flicker`) while the boss is only a shape, and at `--boss-lights` of `--dur-boss-intro` they come on for good and it lands, `bossIntro` in `combatScreen.ts`). Behind the enemy stands the scene of its act (`art/stageArt.ts`, `.stage-art`, inks `--stage-*` in `acts.css`). Before its move lands it tenses up (`hud.ts`: `.winding`, `.wind-hit` for a blow, `.wind-hard` at the end; `CONFIG.anticipate`). A hostile move also *charges* over the last half of its wind-up (`.charging`, an aura in steps via `--charge`), shows a big 3-2-1 count over the enemy (`.count`, sfx `countTick`), and `data-threat` (1 a hit, 2 a heavy one, 3 a lethal one) makes the aura and count bigger and redder.
- `EnemyDef.deepBelt` (the Exaggerated Girl): at that second the engine sets `Combat.lowerHidden` (no stash, no sleeve play, no ability; `lowerSink` event → `.sunk` on the screen: everything under the belt slides down, only the mana bar stays) and `CONFIG.sinkTime` later adds a belt row (`rowAdded` → `.deep`, `--rows` on the belt, the row grows in steps). The first two rows never move: the slide keeps the belt in place (`.sunk .belt` margin = the action row's height), the new row takes that room, and the cards keep the size they had at the start (`startRows` in `combatScreen.ts`). `Combat.beltRows` is therefore mutable.
- **Speech bubbles** (`combat/speech.ts`, `say`): the enemy's lines (`.speech`, over it) and the hero's (`.shout`: *Get to work!*, the ability's name, *Problem solved*; low on the stage, tail down at the hero, so it never covers the bars) are typed out while the speaker's voice reads them; one of each kind at a time.
- **The hero's face** (`components/heroMood.ts`, `art/heroMoods.ts`): the portrait holds the hero's drawing and one drawing per mood (`MOODS`: blink, hurt, tired, cheer, cast; ids like `warriorHurt`, the hero's drawing with its eyes and mouth painted over, each hero's coordinates in `FACES`). `data-mood` on the portrait picks which shows: a blink every few seconds, a wince when hit, a shout on the ability, battered while low on HP, a grin on a win; the portrait also breathes (`idle`). The hero select uses them too (`.faces`): the hero in view says hello, and a tap makes them pull a face. A new hero needs a `FACES` entry (a test checks every hero has every mood).
- **The map and the end**: a fight already won shows the beaten enemy's little portrait on its node (`.node.beaten`); a win ends with the hero hopping and pulling a new face every beat (`DANCE` in `end.ts`), a loss leaves them lying there, battered.
- **Small feedback**: a press that goes on towards a hold shows a ring of eight pixels filling beside the pointer (`holdRing` in `dom.ts`, called by `onPress`, `onTapOrHold` and the card layer; `CONFIG.holdRingDelayMs` keeps taps from showing it). The fight's top bar has the pay on offer (`.pay-chip`, `fightPay` read each frame: a coin a second until par, then dimmed; a hold opens its explanation). The hint under Clock in (*Hold anything to learn what it does*) is written only while `seenTutorial` is false: the very first fight. A card played sounds by its type (`playAttack`, `playDefense`, `playSkill`, `playPower`) and each one played soon after the last climbs a step of `CONFIG.chain` (`sfx(id, pitch)` bends every note of the sound). The handbook's tabs (and the home's Handbook button) carry a dot while a page has found something new since it was opened (`newPages`/`markPageSeen` in `meta.ts`, `meta.pagesSeen`); an empty History shows a desk with a note on it.
- **The time card** (`timeCard` in `combatScreen.ts`) is stamped with the player's own clock (`realClockText`); the workday's clock (`clockAt`) is still what the act intro and the map's boss clock show.
- **Music weight** (`setMusicHeaviness`, `HEAVY` in `audio/music.ts`; `CONFIG.heavyMusic`): as the hero's HP falls from 70% towards 20% the music gets heavier: a low shelf boosts the bass, a sine an octave under the bass line joins it, the kick hits harder, a soft clip adds grit (so the low notes are heard on a phone's speaker) and the top is darkened; it glides there in a moment and goes back to normal when the fight ends or the screen is left.
- **Mana pips** (`hud.ts` `renderMana`): the filling pip grows in `PIP_STEPS`, a pip that fills pops (the last one, at full mana, also throws sparks), and the one that gives a belt card exactly the mana it waits for jumps and flashes (`.unlock`).
- **Rooms come in** (`ENTRANCE` in `components/room.ts`, `.enter-<room>` in `rooms.css`): each room's picture arrives its own way, with sounds timed to it (the Break Room machine slides in, Promotion rises a floor at a time, the Copy Room is scanned in by a bar of light that then goes, the Tailor's curtain draws back, Lost & Found drops with a thud, the Vending Machine flickers on, Restructuring pops in out of true); the idle loops start after.
- `EnemyDef.manaTap` (Conveyor Sis, a surprise): at that second the engine sets `Combat.manaTapOn` (no regeneration, `manaTap` event → `.mana-tapping` on the combat screen, her speech) and every touch of the `.mana-tap` button beside the mana bar calls `Combat.tapMana()` for `CONFIG.manaTapAmount` of a mana (the balance bot taps 4 times a second).
- `EnemyDef.beltOff` (the Power Socket, a surprise): at that second the engine sets `Combat.beltDead` (`beltBoost` is 0, `beltDead` event → `.belt-dead` on the combat screen, its speech) and the belt only moves through `Combat.crankBelt(move)`, in belt widths (clockwise only: a backward turn does nothing; new cards still arrive with the travel, `settleBelt`). The `.crank` knob appears in the action row (`crank.ts`): it follows the finger's angle and one full clockwise turn is `CONFIG.crankTurn` belt widths (the knob never turns back), with a buzz every `CONFIG.crankBuzz` degrees. The track stripes follow `Combat.beltCranked`.
- **Card faces**: the face keeps a thin margin at the sides (`.c-face` padding); a glyph row wider than that shrinks to fit (`ui/components/fitFace.ts`, a `MutationObserver` that sets `--fit` on `.gl` rows as cards are added or their numbers rewritten), so no glyph touches the card's edge.
- **Sleeve nudge**: in the scripted first run (`isFirstRun`), after `CONFIG.sleeveHintAfter` fights in a row that never stashed into or played from the sleeve (`Combat.sleeveUses`, `RunState.sleeveIdle`, counted in `applyCombat`), the next fight's door ends on a coach overlay pointing at the sleeve (`sleeveHint`, combatScreen.ts); once ever (`settings.seenTips` holds `sleeve`).
- The Tourist's luggage: `MoveDef.curse` entries take a `hex` (the `suitcase` one, two taps) and the cards (`carryOn`, `dutyFree`, `snowGlobe`) are `large`.
- **Boss phases** (`EnemyDef.phases`, the Board): the `EnemyDef` itself is phase 1 (`main`, `specials`, `every`, `art`, `belt`); each `EnemyPhase` that follows gives `at` (the share of max HP it starts at), its `art`, its own pattern and its `belt` (`BeltSetting`: rows and a speed multiplier). `Combat.checkDeaths` calls `enterPhase`: the pattern starts again from the top (`Combat.foe` is the pattern in use, never read `def.main` in a fight), `setBeltRows` adds or drops belt rows (cards on a dropped row go to the discard pile; event `rows`, the combat screen measures the cards again: `CARD_MAX_H` has an entry per row count: three rows keep the cards of two on a tall screen and shrink towards a short one, `cardMaxH`), `phaseBeltMul` scales the belt, `EnemyPhase.block` is Block gained on entering (the Board: `BOARD_BLOCK`), and the `phase` event swaps the sprite and says `enemy.<id>.phase<N>` with the note `.phase<N>.d` (`.phase1.d` is only read by the handbook, which lists the moves of every phase under a "Phase N" line).

### Chores (`MoveDef.task`)

A move with a `task` makes the hero do something by hand while it charges: a window (`.task-window`, `combat-task.css`) usually covers the belt, mana bar and sleeve (belt cards, the sleeve and stashing are out of reach: `isCovered`, `lowerBarred`; the ability stays), measured when it opens, and the fight runs on underneath. A chore that is played on the belt itself has `covers = false` and no window. Done in time, the move is skipped and the enemy is stunned for the chore's `calm` seconds; otherwise the hit lands. A mistake (`wrong`) takes the chore's `penalty` s off the move's countdown (`penaltyMax` in all) and only shakes and flashes the window, never re-runs its entrance. The pure state is seeded by `combat.rng` and ticked by simulated time; the enemy says `enemy.<id>.order` when it opens and `.calm` when it is done. The test bot does each chore.

`Combat.chore` is the one chore on (a `Chore`, `game/chore.ts`: `id`, `covers`, `phase`, `errors`, `fined`, `tick`, and `serve` for one that deals the belt's cards); `CHORES` makes it from the move's `TaskId`, and the hand's moves go through the chore's own entry point (`Combat.coffee`, `Combat.pickShell`, `Combat.eat`, which fine a `wrong` one by `CONFIG[chore.id]`). In the UI `taskFrame.ts` owns the window every chore shares (title bar, open/leave life cycle, position over the belt); each chore's file draws what is inside. **A new chore** = a class with an `id`, an entry in `TaskId` and `CHORES`, `CONFIG.<id>` (`calm`, `penalty`, `penaltyMax`…), a window on `createTaskFrame` (or, if it covers nothing, its own small element like `sushiSlip.ts`), the bot's move in `tests/bot.ts`, `rule.<id>` strings, and `enemy.<id>.order`/`.calm` for the enemy that sets it.

- **Coffee** (`'coffee'`, the Slaves CEO's *Get the Boss Coffee*; `game/coffee.ts`, `coffeeWindow.ts`, `CONFIG.coffee`): key in the drink's code on the pad, pay the price in euro coins (the slot takes a coin only if the purse can still make up the price: `fits`), put the cup and then the spoon, in that order (not the fork), under the spout (the three keys lie on the tray in a shuffled order: `CoffeeTask.tray`), dial the sugar and press start; the pour (`brewTime`) and the served cup (`doneHold`, celebrated, then the window shrinks away) end it. Coins and the cup can be dragged or tapped; the spoon before the cup is a mistake like the fork. Moves go through `Combat.coffee(action)`.
- **Shell game** (`'shells'`, the Board's Payroll Audit; `game/shells.ts`, `shellWindow.ts`, `CONFIG.shells`): covered cards show their faces, turn over and swap places (`ShellGame`; `swap` is the pair under way for the animation), then one is picked with `Combat.pickShell(place)`; a wrong one is shown, costs seconds and the round restarts.
- **Sushi order** (`'sushi'`, the Sushi Chef's Omakase; `game/sushi.ts`, `sushiSlip.ts`, `CONFIG.sushi`): two slips (`slips`, one in each top corner of the stage), each an order of `length` pieces: a dish (kinds of sushi, repeats allowed) on a coloured plate (`CombatCard.plate`, shown as `data-plate`: the card art takes the plate's ink). A piece serves whichever slip asks for it now, in that slip's order, so the slips are worked in parallel (`SushiOrder.wanted`, `wants`). The belt serves nothing but sushi meanwhile (`serve`: mostly pieces the slips still want and the belt hasn't got; the share of random plates starts at `decoy` and grows with every piece eaten, and `fugu` of them are the trap) and runs faster with every piece eaten (`Chore.beltMul`, from `beltFrom` to `beltTo`; the music follows). Your own cards on the belt and in the sleeve stay in reach. Tap the pieces (`Combat.eat`); a wrong plate (wrong dish or wrong colour) stays on the belt and costs seconds and `wrongHp` HP, so does a piece a slip asks for riding off (it comes round again), the fugu bites (`play`) and is gone, and the last piece clears the plates.
- **Badge swipe** (`'badge'`, the Punch Clock's Badge Swipe; `game/badge.ts`, `badgeWindow.ts`, `CONFIG.badge`): drag the badge through the reader's slot, not too slow and not too fast. The UI times the middle of the track and hands `Combat.swipeBadge` a speed in tracks per second; the reader takes `centre ± tolerance`. A swipe that is too slow or too fast is refused, so is the first `glitches` ([min, max]) that would have passed (`glitch`, with a random excuse from `GLITCHES`: it never works first time), and after every refusal the zone drifts (`drift`). Every refusal costs seconds and `wrongHp` HP. The window shows the zone and the speed of the finger on a gauge; the phone hums while the speed is right and buzzes when it is too fast (`haptic('swipe')`, `'swipeRough'`).
- **Self-Checkout** (`'checkout'`, the Self-Checkout's Unexpected Item; `game/checkout.ts`, `checkoutWindow.ts`, `CONFIG.checkout`): a basket of `items` groceries (`GROCERIES`) to scan one by one into the bagging area (`CheckoutLane.scan`). After some of the scans (`trips`, pre-rolled) the machine objects to the item just bagged (`phase` `unexpected`): nothing can be scanned until it is lifted out (`lift`, back to the basket), and then the machine asks for `assist` s of patience (`phase` `assist`) in which touching anything is a mistake. Done when the basket is empty. The hand goes through `Combat.checkout`; a mistake costs seconds and `wrongHp` HP.
- **Barbecue** (`'grill'`, the Barbecue Dad's Flip the Burgers; `game/grill.ts`, `grillWindow.ts`, `CONFIG.grill`): a grate of `slots` patties (`GrillLine.slots`), `patties` to serve in all. A side takes `cook` s to be ready, stays ready for `window` s, then burns (`stateOf`: `raw` / `ready` / `burnt`; the window's gauge draws the three and a marker). Tap a ready side to flip it (`Combat.grill(slot)`), the second ready side serves the burger; a tap on a raw or burnt patty is a mistake (a burnt one is binned and replaced). Patties start `stagger` s apart; the grate never holds more than are left to serve. Time-driven (`tick`), so it is the only chore that needs a clock of its own.

### Minigames (`EnemyDef.minigame`)

A minigame is a game of its own that an enemy leaves open to the hero, instead of a chore a move sets: a button beside the enemy (`.minigame-btn`, in `.enemy-wrap`) opens and closes its window whenever the hero likes, the fight never waits for it, and what it scores takes HP off the enemy. `Combat.minigame` is the game (a `Minigame`, `game/minigame.ts`; `MINIGAMES` makes it from the `MinigameId`, with its own `Rng` taken from `combat.rng` once, so playing it never changes the cards the deck deals), `Combat.minigameOpen` whether its window is up and `Combat.openMinigame(open)` the button. While the window is up it covers the belt and the sleeve the way a chore's does (`isCovered`, `lowerBarred`; the ability stays). The game is turn-based and has no clock, so closing it costs nothing and it is found as it was left. The enemy's `enemy.<id>.game` line (a trait in the handbook and before the fight, `enemyTraits`) says how it works. **A new minigame** = a class with an `id`, an entry in `MinigameId` and `MINIGAMES`, `CONFIG.<id>`, a window on `createTaskFrame` that draws `Combat.minigame` and calls the game's own entry point on `Combat`, the bot's play in `tests/bot.ts`, and `task.<id>.*` strings; when a second one has the same shape, `Minigame` and `matchSwap` get a common entry point.

- **Backlog** (`'match3'`, the Supervisor's; `game/match3.ts`, `match3Window.ts`, `CONFIG.match3`): a match-3 on a `cols`×`rows` board of `kinds` kinds of office stuff (`KIND_ICONS`), 1000 HP of backlog. Swap two neighbours (a tap on each, or a drag towards one) with `Combat.matchSwap(a, b)`; only a swap that lines up `MATCH_LINE` or more of a kind (or has a special in it) is allowed, anything else leaves the board as it was. `MatchThree.swap` works the whole move out at once, round by round (the lines go, the specials among them go off, what is above falls, new pieces come in, until the board is quiet), and hands back the rounds for the window to play (`--dur-match` a step; closing the window mid-way only skips the show). Every piece cleared takes `tileHp` HP off the enemy at once (a share of his max HP when the floor made him tougher, so every floor asks for the same moves) and each cascade after the first pays `combo` more. `STAMP_LINE` in a line leave a stamp (clears the row or column across the line), `COPIER_LINE` a copier (swapped with a piece, it takes every piece of that kind); a board with no swap left is shuffled. The balance bot keeps the window open 6 s of every 9 and makes a move every 1.6 s.

## Relics

`RelicDef` (`data/relics.ts`): `mods` (sleeve, maxMana, regen, `beltSpeed` a multiplier, `abilityCost` added to the hero's ability cost (`abilityCostWith`: Cheat Sheet), `startBelt` how far in the belt has run when the fight opens, `rewardCards` extra cards on offer: the reward grid widens with `--swap-n`) and `hooks` (`onCombatStart`, `onCardPlayed`, `onCardExpired`, `onDeath`, `damageMult` for the hero's card damage, `cancelHit` to turn an enemy hit away, `wipeCurse` to wipe a curse card an enemy move deals…), flags such as `openPool` (offers draw from every class) and `restBoth` (a Break Room gives the nap and the training: the room stays open until both are done or spent), `n` = the number its text shows. Each has a `rarity` (`special` = the Tailor's): the handbook's Stationery page lists them by rising rarity with the card-style corner; the Lost & Found box deals each from `relicOdds(act)` (`config.ts`), holds at least `relicGuarantee(act)` and never two of one rarity (`rollRelics`, `run.ts`). A relic that triggers every so often has `progress` (0..1): the fight shows it as a chip with a filling bar in the hero's status row. A relic that waits for one trigger (once a fight or a run) has `armed`: its chip is lit while it waits and dimmed once spent. **Every relic with a trigger that is not instant needs one of the two**, so the player always sees it before it goes off. A hook shows itself with a `relic` event (floating name). Besides the first ones, `RelicHooks` has `cost` (changes what a card costs, last step of `Combat.costOf`: Just-in-Time Delivery halves, rounded up, a belt card in the last `JIT_LAST` of the belt), `anchorCard` (pins a belt card that reached the end, like an Anchor card, `Combat.relicAnchors`: the Safety Net's first cards of a fight, never a curse, an unplayable card or a plate of sushi), `onCurse` (a curse an enemy move dealt: the Revenge Spreadsheet's Strength) and `onHitAbsorbed` (Block took a whole enemy hit: the Bulletproof Cardigan's mana). Flexitime's clock stops while a Lost Badge is up; the Locker Key writes `CombatCard.cut` (a discount that lasts the fight) on a card that falls; the Assorted Biscuit Tin hits on the first live tick, so the hit is seen; the Stock Options' `onGain` takes `STOCK_HP` of the max HP when they are taken (the mana is a plain `mods.maxMana`). Needs a sprite in `art/relics.ts` (keyed by its id, drawn like a creature, most with a cute face), `relic.<id>.name`/`.d`. Run state: `run.relics` (ids) and `run.relicFlags` (once-per-run flags); the hero sheet lists them.

## Career ladder (ranks)

`data/rank.ts` (`LADDER`: one list of ten `RankStep`s for each hero) and `src/ui/components/rank.ts`. Every rung is one perk (`RankBonus`) and one duty (`RankDuty`), typed unions of effects with an `n`; a run plays under all the rungs up to its rank, so a lower rank is an easier shift. Rank 0 is the plain game (`NO_MODS`). The ladders share the effects but not the order: each hero's duties go for what it leans on (the Mage's HP and tempo, the Necromancer's sleeve and HP, the Rogue's sleeve), its perks feed what it lacks (the Necromancer starts with two mana), and the ability price, the cards of a rank's gift and the starter cards that start upgraded are the hero's own. The ladders were tuned with the sim bot (`BotOpts.level`): the middle of a ladder is easier than the plain game, the top harder.

- **Progress** (`meta.ranks[hero]`: `top`, `seen`, `pick`; validated field by field in `meta.ts`): `finishRun` calls `promoteRank(hero, run.level)` when the run clocked out of the last main act, or died in the weekend after it, and its rank was the hero's top one; it raises `top` (and `pick`) and `RunEnd.promoted` says so (the end screen's note, tap → hire screen). Rank titles are `rank.0`…`rank.10` (the same for every hero), effect lines `rank.fx.<kind>` (`rank.fx.cards.<rarity>` for a gift of cards), shown through `keywordHtml`.
- **In the run**: `RunState.level` (saved, `SAVE_VERSION`; `newRun(…, level)`). The HP, mana and sleeve a hero starts with at a rank are `rankStart` (the run is dealt them, the hire screen shows them). `grantRank` (`run.ts`) hands the other start-of-run gifts over once: mana crystals, upgraded starter cards, a stationery dealt like the Lost & Found's (`rollRelics`), random cards of a rarity from the hero's reward pool (`rewardPool`, class weight as in a reward). What plays out later is summed into `Mods` (`rankMods`, read as `runMods(run)`): `restHeal`, `eliteHp`, `enemyHp`/`enemyDmg`, `beltMul`, `rewardCards`, `manaSlow` (a faster refill is a `manaRegen` perk), `blockFade`, `enemyTime`, `abilityCost` (a fixed price), `sleeve` (`sleeveCut` takes slots, never the last), `restBoth`, `noBeg`, `noSkipPay`, and `hard`, which makes the adaptive pressure climb less (`CONFIG.pressure.rankUp`). `combatSetup` passes the ones the fight reads (`abilityCost`, `sleeveBonus`, `manaSlow`, `blockFade`, `enemyTime`, `canBeg`); `enemyScale(node, mods, pressure)` takes `Mods` (`eliteHp` only for elites and bosses). Analytics tag a ranked run apart (`plain`, `-rank`), the payslip and the history carry the rank (`RunLog.rank`).
- **The hire screen**: a brass plaque under the hero's job (`rankPlaque`: the number and the title of the rank picked; not in the very first run) opens the ladder window (`openLadder`: every rank with its perk and duty, the reached ones tappable to pick, the rest locked and dimmed, `meta.rankPick`). The HP, mana and sleeve chips and the ability's price follow the picked rank (`paint` in `heroSelect.ts`). A promotion not shown yet (`rankPromotion`: `top` above `seen`) is played once on the plaque of the hero in view, a beat after the screen opens or the hero comes into view (`playPromotion`: the plaque shakes with the old rank, then the new number is slammed in under a *Promoted!* stamp and a burst of confetti; `--dur-promote-*` tokens), and `markRankSeen` ends it. With the debug menus on, a bug button at the top right (`openRankDebug`) sets each hero's top rank (Promote plays the climb, Demote, Unlock all titles, Reset) and builds the screen again.
- **A new ladder** = a `LADDER` entry (ten rungs: a test checks the texts, the gifts and that the upgraded starter cards exist); a new effect = a member of `RankBonus`/`RankDuty`, its case in `apply` (or `grantRank`/`rankStart`), its `rank.fx.<kind>` line in every language.

## Adaptive difficulty (pressure)

Invisible to the player; `game/pressure.ts` (pure) and `CONFIG.pressure`. A run that is `hard` (a rank above 0) cuts its climb (`rankUp`). `RunState.pressure` holds two levels from -1 to 1: `hp` (the next enemies' HP) and `dmg` (their damage). `applyCombat` feeds them after every **won** fight (`pressureLearns`: every fight counts, from the first one, except in the scripted first run) with the `FightFacts` of the `Combat` (`fightFacts`). `fightSignals` reads two things: the damage the player dealt per second (the enemy's HP lost plus the Block it soaked up, over the fight's time: it doesn't depend on the enemy's size or on the pressure's own HP) against `par[tier].dps` (more = positive, drives `hp`), and the HP the hero lost (the damage it took minus what it healed in the fight), as a share of max HP, against `par[tier].loss` (less = positive, drives `dmg`); a stretch of `dead` around par says nothing, a signal is full at `spread` away from it. `learn` moves each level toward its signal by `rate[tier]` (elites and bosses say more), so on target the levels drift back to 0; a hero who **begged to stay** in the fight (`Combat.begged`) sends both levels to -1 instead, and the next fights move them from there; a win that leaves the hero under `closeCall` of its max HP (`FightFacts.left`) does the same to `dmg` alone. `pressureScale` turns the levels into multipliers (up to +`max.up`, down to -`max.down`: it climbs more than it eases, and a full ease-off is -10%; at a rank above 0 a climb is cut to `rankUp` of that), and `enemyScale` multiplies them in with the floor and rank scaling, every enemy tier included. A loss ends the run, so it never teaches. A run saved by another version is dropped, so the field needs no migration. The `par` numbers are the sim bot's medians of won fights, so retune them from real play. The debug menus of the map and of the fight show the levels and their multipliers (`pressureInfo`), and the fight recap shows what that fight said and the levels it moved to.

## Analytics, reviews, crash reports

- **Analytics** (`src/analytics/`): anonymous counters (no id, no deck, no seed), sent as GoatCounter events (`<version>/fight/<enemy>/<hero>/win|lose`, `offered/<card>`, `pick/<card>`, `cut/<card>`, `skip`, `run/<hero>/win|lose|abandon[-rank]`, `death/act<N>-floor<M>`). Called only from the flow (`main.ts`, the reward screen), never from the engine. Runs at a rank above 0 send only their tagged run result. The endpoint is `VITE_STATS_URL` (repository variable `STATS_URL` in the Pages workflow); unset = nothing is sent (dev, tests). Settings switch `analytics` (on by default) turns it off; turning on the debug menus switches it off too. Sampled data: ad blockers hide some players. To swap the service rewrite `goatcounter.ts`; to remove it delete the folder, the call sites, the setting and its strings.
- **Reviews**: once `CONFIG.ratingAfterRuns` runs are over (`meta.history`) and none has been sent (`meta.rated`), the home shows a `.rate-banner` with five stars (`ratingDue`, `title.ts`); a star opens the review window (`reviewModal.ts`: stars, free text up to `CONFIG.reviewMax`, Send). `src/feedback/firebase.ts` files it in the Firestore collection `reviews` (REST, no SDK: `stars`, `text`, `version`, `locale`, `at`); the project is `VITE_FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID`/`VITE_FIREBASE_API_KEY` (repository variables `FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID`/`FIREBASE_API_KEY` in the Pages workflow; the key is public by design). Unset = nothing is sent. Firestore rules must allow only `create` on `reviews` (no read, update or delete). On failure the window stays open and says so.
- **Crash reports**: `catchCrashes` (`main.ts`) also calls `sendCrash(report)` (`firebase.ts`): the same text as the Machine jam's Copy button (version, browser, stack, cut at `CONFIG.crashMax`) goes, fire and forget, to the Firestore collection `crashes` (`report`, `version`, `locale`, `at`; rules: only `create`). It follows the `analytics` switch. To remove: delete `sendCrash`, its call and import, `CONFIG.crashMax`, and this paragraph.
